# 大剑 · 武器描述与故事

状态：中英描述与故事讨论稿 v0.1 · 2026-10-26

按现有六个独立外观阶段组织 +10～+70 的中英描述与故事。每阶分别保存材质、用途与力量的描述和约一百至二百字的中文故事，英文逐段对应。沿用贴近持剑者、省略主角人称称谓的写法，不逐阶安排更换兵器；古炉、龙骸、熔脉与炉心沿同一柄剑逐渐显现。

本线从北方矿镇与守路者的经历起笔，通过重新开矿、控制粮道、强征和焚城表现统治的形成。灾难中的起点不替后来的暴行辩解，剑的异化也不代替持剑者作出选择。具体姓名、年代与更早身世仍留白，不采用“黄金律法”设定。

三条路线的强化节点不等同于同一日期：大剑 +40 回应骑士剑 +40 的石桥断后，+50 回应骑士剑 +60 的夜袭及圣光，+60～+70 汇入王都守城与最后的挑战。共有事件的结果保持一致；六阶末尾止于双方将要交战，不写定封印是否完成或谁最终归来。

## +10 · 铁匠大剑

参考图：[铁匠大剑](../../display/Greatsword/+10Greatsword.png)

### 武器描述

以厚实银钢锻成的双手大剑。宽直剑身保留锤打与使用的痕迹，黑铁护手横展，加长皮柄中段以铁箍收紧，沉重剑首平衡长刃。适合劈开木盾、拦截来刃与扼守狭道，挥斩需要稳固步法和足够空间。仍是一柄能在普通炉火中修整的铁匠作品。

### Weapon description

A two-handed greatsword forged from thick silversteel. Hammer marks and wear remain on the broad, straight blade. A black iron crossguard spans the long leather grip, reinforced at its centre by an iron band, with a heavy pommel to balance the blade. Suited to breaking wooden shields, intercepting blades and holding narrow roads. Its sweeping cuts require firm footing and room. Still a smith's work, repairable at an ordinary forge.

### 故事片段

北方的矿镇入冬便封山。守路的雇兵拿走最后一袋粮食，运铁的车还堵在关隘。带着矿工拆下路障，守了三天，第一支商队才从雪里进来。

报酬按人头分，伤者的份额放在铁匠铺里。有人把夺来的关印送到桌上，旁边压着几封寄往王庭、始终没有回音的求援信。

入夜后，挨家收齐还能握剑的人，将旧甲与长矛分下去。铁匠在门外试过大剑的刃口，天亮前，关隘换上了新的旗。

### Story fragment

Winter closed the mountain roads around the northern mining town. The hired guards took the last sack of grain; iron carts still waited at the toll gate. With the miners, the barricade pulled down. Three days holding the road before the first caravan emerged from the snow.

Pay divided equally, the wounded fighters' shares left at the smithy. Someone brought the captured toll seal to the table. Beside it lay several appeals sent to the royal court, still unanswered.

After dark, every household searched for those who could still hold a sword. Old armour and spears handed out. The smith tested the greatsword's edge outside the door. Before dawn, a new flag flew over the gate.

## +20 · 烬炉大剑

参考图：[烬炉大剑](../../display/Greatsword/+20Greatsword.png)

### 武器描述

染上古炉余火的银钢大剑。剑根浮出暗红烙印，赤铜色镶边贴着厚重刃身，细小裂纹间留有难以散尽的热。挥斩时能将灼热带入盾面与伤口，烬火使刃口在寒地仍保持温热。双手握柄沿用朴素皮革，承接着凡铁尚未褪尽的形制。

### Weapon description

A silversteel greatsword steeped in the embers of an ancient furnace. A dark red brand has appeared near the blade's base, copper-coloured edging following the heavy steel. Heat lingers in fine cracks. Its cuts carry that heat into shields and wounds, the embers keeping the edge warm even in frozen country. Plain leather still covers the two-handed grip, retaining the shape of its mortal beginnings.

### 故事片段

山雪封住粮道，将关隘留下的人编成守军，重开废弃的矿井。最深处的石门后，古炉仍有赤光，几块矿石放进去，没添炭便烧成了铁水。

炉房彻夜开工，镇上第一次存下过冬的铁锭。送来的军械也堆进院里，矿道外开始有人跪着求见，带来粮食与相邻关卡的消息。

塌方后，有人请求停炉撤人。将停工的木牌扔进火里，另派守军看住井口。领粮的名册旁，从此多了一栏每日交铁的数目。

### Story fragment

Snow sealed the grain roads. Those left at the gate formed a garrison; the abandoned mine reopened. Behind the deepest stone door, red light still burned in an ancient furnace. Ore placed inside ran molten without fresh charcoal.

The forge worked through the night. For the first time, iron ingots were laid by for winter. Weapons arriving for work filled the yard too. Outside the workings, petitioners began to kneel, bringing grain and news of neighbouring gates.

After a collapse, a request to stop the furnace and withdraw the workers. The suspension board thrown into the fire, soldiers posted at the shaft mouth instead. Beside the grain roll, a new column: each day's iron due.

## +30 · 渊角龙骸

参考图：[渊角龙骸](../../display/Greatsword/+30Greatsword.png)

### 武器描述

与深渊龙骸相合的黑铁重剑。古龙骨鳞覆住加宽的剑身，齿状刃缘从层叠甲片间突出，弯曲护手如一对渊角。赤色熔脉沿剑脊隐现，将冲击与灼烧留在劈开的裂口。剑体越发沉重，适于破盾与压开军阵，握柄上的旧皮仍露在骨甲之外。

### Weapon description

A black iron greatsword joined with abyssal dragon remains. Ancient bone and scales cover the widened blade; toothed edges project between layered plates, the curved guard rising like abyssal horns. Red molten veins glimmer along the spine, leaving force and heat in the wounds it opens. A growing weight for breaking shields and pressing ranks apart. The old leather grip remains exposed beneath its bone armour.

### 故事片段

清开塌下的矿壁，下面露出一座龙骸。铁链穿过颈骨，把最深处的石门一点点拖开，送下去的灯火接连熄灭，只有巨大的胸腔里仍在发红。

将佩剑插进骨隙，赤光沿刃身爬上手甲。再拔出来，剑脊已附着细鳞，掌心被烫开的伤口迟迟没有流血。井口围满人，最先跪下的还是守关时的旧部。

一同分过报酬的领队拦住矿车，请求放停工的人回家。解下他的关印，交给身后的兵，把矿车继续推向炉房。

### Story fragment

The collapsed rock cleared, a dragon's skeleton lay beneath. Chains passed through the neck bones, dragging the deepest stone door open inch by inch. Lamps sent below went out in succession. Only the enormous ribcage still glowed red.

The sword driven between the bones. Red light climbed the blade into the gauntlet. Drawn out again, fine scales had fastened to its spine. The burns in the palm would not bleed. People crowded the shaft; the first to kneel were old comrades from the gate.

A leader who had once shared the pay blocked an ore cart, asking leave for the idle workers to go home. His gate seal unfastened and handed to the soldiers behind. The cart pushed onward to the furnace.

## +40 · 熔脉龙骸

参考图：[熔脉龙骸](../../display/Greatsword/+40Greatsword.png)

### 武器描述

熔脉贯通龙骸的巨剑，黑铁与骨鳞层层包覆宽刃，炽红裂隙从剑尖汇向护手深处尚在孕育的核心。弯曲龙角将热量聚在剑根，挥击可使甲片熔开，烬火落上木石后仍难扑灭。旧时的刃线已被骨甲吞没，整柄剑仿佛仍在沿着血脉生长。

### Weapon description

A dragonbone greatsword threaded through with molten veins. Black iron and bone scales sheath the broad blade; red fissures converge from the point upon a core still forming within the guard. Curved horns gather the heat at its base. Blows can melt armour, the embers clinging stubbornly to wood and stone. Bone plates have swallowed the old outline. The sword seems to grow along its own veins.

### 故事片段

北境诸城的降书铺满长桌。城主交出钥匙，仍有人不肯交粮。将封着粮仓的门连同守门人一起烧开，第二日，降兵便按新占的城池编队，旧纹章涂成黑色。

大军在河口被石桥拦住三昼夜。赤火沿桥板烧过去，追到河边，最后一队伤兵已经到了对岸，只剩断木带着火漂远。

把桥两侧的村庄划进征粮的范围，命人沿河搜船。军吏捧来封王的冠冕，先将它放在烧焦的城印上，再接过下一座城的道路图。

### Story fragment

The northern cities' surrenders covered the long table. Lords yielded keys; some still refused grain. The sealed granary doors burned open, their guards with them. The next day, surrendered soldiers formed units by the cities taken, old crests painted black.

At the river mouth, a stone bridge held the army for three days and nights. Red fire ran along its boards. By the time the pursuit reached the bank, the last wounded had crossed. Only burning timbers drifted away.

The villages on both banks marked for grain levies, boats to be seized along the river. An officer presented a coronation crown. First laid upon the charred city seals, then the road map to the next city taken up.

## +50 · 深渊炉心

参考图：[深渊炉心](../../display/Greatsword/+50Greatsword.png)

### 武器描述

炉心彻底苏醒的龙骸巨剑。炽红核心显露于护手中央，黑铁甲片与龙翼般的侧刃围住火光，熔岩裂纹贯穿整段剑身。核心持续催发深渊赤火，使劈斩既能破甲，也能将灼热泼向前列军阵；持剑者的血与火已沿握柄相通，炉心的搏动在握持时清晰可觉。

### Weapon description

A dragonbone greatsword with its furnace heart fully awake. The red core stands exposed at the guard's centre, enclosed by black iron plates and wing-like blades. Molten fissures run the length of the weapon. The heart continually feeds abyssal red fire, adding bursts of heat to armour-breaking blows. Blood and flame now meet through the grip; the core's pulse can be felt in the hand.

### 故事片段

渡口附近扎营的那夜，敌军沿废弃的小路越过壕沟。帐外号声未齐，一柄圣剑已经逼近。拖起巨剑，护手里的炉心第一次完整搏动，赤火涌过前列，将骑士的盾接连熔穿。

倒下的统领身后，有人折返接住那柄剑。蓝石亮起，白光从火里撑出一道缺口，伤兵沿着残旗退出营地。再次举剑时，炉心的震动已经与胸口合在一起。

战后清点缴来的甲胄，将还没熄灭的火收进铁盆。伤口不再流出红血，袖口与握柄之间，只有一线暗火缓缓游动。

### Story fragment

At the camp near the ferry, enemy troops crossed the ditch by an abandoned path in the night. Before the alarm had spread, a sacred sword was already close. The greatsword dragged up. Within its guard, the furnace heart beat fully for the first time. Red fire poured over the front rank, melting the knights' shields one after another.

Behind the fallen commander, someone turned back for the blade. A blue stone brightened. White light pressed a gap through the fire, the wounded withdrawing behind a torn banner. As the greatsword rose again, the heart's pulse had joined the beat within the chest.

After battle, captured armour counted and the lingering fire gathered into iron bowls. Wounds no longer shed red blood. Between sleeve and hilt, only a narrow thread of dark fire moved.

## +60～+70 · 余烬君王

参考图：[余烬君王](../../display/Greatsword/+60Greatsword.png)

### 武器描述

与黑暗君王共享称谓的终阶巨剑。龙骸与黑铁铸成宽阔刃身，金色甲饰沿熔脉收束，双龙展开的翼骨环抱炽红炉心。深渊之火循着剑脊不断涌出，足以熔穿重盾、点燃城楼，握持者的意志也随火延伸至战场。核心尚在搏动时，剑上的余烬便不会自然冷却。

### Weapon description

The final greatsword sharing its title with the dark king. Dragon remains and black iron form the broad blade, golden armour tracing its molten veins. The spread wing bones of twin dragons enclose the red furnace heart. Abyssal fire pours along the spine, melting heavy shields and setting towers alight; the wielder's will reaches across the field with it. While the heart beats, the weapon's embers do not cool of their own accord.

### 故事片段

大军压到王都，守军的旗从城前连到墙下。举剑催火，白光却沿着盾列一次次展开。第三日，阵地终于空出。城门久攻不下，墙后的锤声又接着响了七夜。

军帐前堆满降城送来的金器。戴上冠冕，遣回求和的使者，命人堵住仍在外逃的驿路。箭手排进黑旗之下，炉心把夜色映成赤红。

天将明时，一名骑士迎着箭雨走来，肩头的箭杆折断，脚步仍未停下。那柄白剑外环着金光。压住重新张弦的弓手，拖起巨剑，剑锋迎着来人抬起。

### Story fragment

The army pressed upon the capital. Defenders' flags stretched from the field to the walls. Fire summoned with the blade, but white light kept spreading along the shield ranks. On the third day, the field finally emptied. The gates held. Behind the walls, hammers sounded for another seven nights.

Gold from surrendered cities piled before the command tent. Crown on the brow, the envoys seeking peace sent away. Orders to block the post roads still carrying fugitives. Archers formed beneath black banners. The furnace heart turned the night red.

Near dawn, a knight came through the arrows. The shaft in the shoulder broken short, the advance unhalted. Gold light circled a white blade. A hand checked the archers drawing again. The greatsword dragged up, its edge raised to meet the approaching figure.
