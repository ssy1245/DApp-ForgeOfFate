# 骑士剑 · 武器描述与故事

状态：中英描述与故事整合讨论稿 v0.7 · 2026-10-26

按现有九个外观阶段组织 +0～+90 的中英武器描述与连续故事。每阶分别保存“武器描述 / Weapon description”和“故事片段 / Story fragment”，附对应参考图链接；前端按当前语言读取。描述以图片为依据，结合故事设定介绍材质、形制、用途与力量；中文故事各约一百至二百字，英文逐段对应。两种语言均贴近持剑者的视角，省略主角的人称称谓，以出身、军旅生活、人物行动和战事推进经历。不引入青梅支线，不采用“黄金律法”设定。

前半段不逐阶安排换剑：家乡的旧剑随持剑者从军、立功、受封。+50 铺垫统领与历代誓言，+60 在战场接过阵亡统领的圣剑，继承其英灵与职责。+70 全军誓言汇入同一柄剑，+80 举国重铸时保留圣石与历代誓词。每节讲述当时的经历，不以交剑、筹料等升级过程作过渡。

自 +40 起进入抵抗余烬君王的战争，石桥、赤火、龙骸巨剑与不灭炉心可供巨剑路线从另一立场回应。英灵的神力真实存在；最后的黎明为举国铸成的镇魔剑，执剑者的献身是封印的代价。结尾停在举剑挑战暴君、决斗尚未开始之时，最终结果留白。

## +0～+9 · 余烬长剑

参考图：[余烬长剑](../../display/BasicSword/+0BasicSword.png)

### 武器描述

普通铁料锻成的旧长剑。宽直的剑身布满锈斑与磨痕，短护手残留火燎的暗色，握柄上的旧皮已经开裂。双刃仍能重新磨利，适合练习基本劈刺，也能用于乡间护身。厚实的剑身经得住修补，磨去浮锈后，依旧是一把能握稳的兵器。

### Weapon description

An old longsword forged from common iron. Rust and wear mark its broad, straight blade; fire has darkened the short guard, and the leather grip is cracked. Both edges can still be sharpened for practice or protection on country roads. The sturdy blade has endured many repairs. Beneath the rust, it remains a serviceable weapon.

### 故事片段

村子靠着北境的驿道，入冬前总有骑士团驻扎。替他们牵马、擦拭盔甲，能换几枚铜钱。父亲打了一辈子农具，家里唯一的长剑，是去年炉房失火后，从灰堆里扒出来的旧货。

磨去刃上的浮锈，白天拉风箱，晚上练驻军教过的几个招式。院后的木桩换过三回，剑柄上的旧皮也缠了一层又一层。起初练上十几下便抬不起胳膊，后来能从晚饭后练到村里的灯一盏盏熄灭。

### Story fragment

The village stood beside the northern post road. Knights always came to billet before winter. Leading their horses and cleaning their armour earned a few copper coins. Father had spent a lifetime forging farm tools. The only longsword at home was an old blade pulled from the ashes after last year's forge fire.

The surface rust came off. Days at the bellows; evenings practising the few strokes taught by the garrison. The practice post behind the house had been replaced three times, the grip wrapped in fresh layers of leather. At first, a dozen cuts left no strength to raise the blade. In time, practice lasted from supper until the village lamps went out, one by one.

## +10 · 精铁骑士剑

参考图：[精铁骑士剑](../../display/KnightSword/+10KnightSword.png)

### 武器描述

以精炼铁料反复锻打的军用长剑。厚实的剑脊贯至尖端，平直护手与八角剑首均以铁制成，皮革握柄用细绳缠紧。形制是军中常见的样式，兼顾劈砍、刺击与格挡，适合操练和长途随军使用；刃口受损后，可在营中磨修。

### Weapon description

A military longsword hammered from refined iron. A sturdy central ridge runs to the point; the straight guard and eight-sided pommel are iron, the leather grip bound tightly with cord. A familiar pattern in the ranks, suited to cuts, thrusts and parries, daily drill and long campaigns. Damaged edges can be ground back into service at camp.

### 故事片段

名字登记在远征军的名册末尾，领到一件短甲和一卷铺盖。军需官验过从家里带来的佩剑，在剑鞘上烙下营号，身后的队伍又往前挪了一步。

营里住着铁匠、农夫和赶车人的儿子。挤在同一顶帐篷里吃饭、补衣，轮流替夜里站岗的人留下半碗热汤。出发那天，背着行囊排进长队，跟着前面的脚印翻过山口。

天未亮便随号声起身，白天赶路，扎营后还要列队操练。起初总被剑鞘绊住脚，后来连睡下时，也把剑放在伸手能碰到的地方。

### Story fragment

A name entered at the foot of the expeditionary muster roll. A short coat of mail and a bedroll issued. The quartermaster inspected the sword brought from home and branded the unit's number into the scabbard. The queue behind shuffled forward another step.

The camp housed the sons of smiths, farmers and carters. Meals and mending in the same tent; half a bowl of hot soup set aside for whoever stood the night watch. On departure day, a pack on the back and a place in the long column, following the footprints over the mountain pass.

Reveille before dawn, marching by day, drill after making camp. At first, the scabbard kept catching between the feet. Later, even at night, the sword lay within reach.

## +20 · 银誓长剑

参考图：[银誓长剑](../../display/KnightSword/+20KnightSword.png)

### 武器描述

以银钢锻成的行伍佩剑，双刃细磨，剑身兼具硬度与韧性，适合反复接战。护手与剑首各嵌一枚蓝石，细金饰边沿十字护手展开，保留了便于持握的深色皮柄。蓝石象征军旗下的誓言，常见于久经远征的老兵和领队者腰间。

### Weapon description

A campaign sword forged from silversteel, its finely ground edges supported by a blade both hard and resilient. Blue gems sit in the guard and pommel, with fine gold edging along the crossguard and dark leather around the grip. The gems signify the oath sworn beneath the colours. A sword often carried by veterans and leaders of small companies.

### 故事片段

远征第二年，渡河的队伍遭了伏击。折回河滩接上掉队的伤兵，沿芦苇荡走了一夜，直到天亮才看见营旗。回营点名，同行的人一个不少，名字也被添进受赏的名单。

赏银寄了一半回家，剩下的分给一道下河的人。军饷多领了一份，帐里分来几个新兵，出操时跟在身后。

授赏那日，在军旗下重念入伍的誓词。回帐时，新兵正围着磨刀石坐成一圈，便从握剑和收步开始，逐个纠正。次日行军，把走得最慢的留在身前。

### Story fragment

In the expedition's second year, the river crossing was ambushed. Back to the bank for the wounded stragglers, then a night through the reeds before the camp flags appeared at dawn. At roll call, everyone brought along was accounted for. A name added to the honours list.

Half the reward sent home; the rest divided among those who had gone back down to the river. An extra share of pay, and several recruits assigned to the tent. At drill, they now followed behind.

On the day of the award, the enlistment oath repeated beneath the colours. Back at the tent, recruits sat around the whetstone. Grip and footwork corrected, one at a time. On the next march, the slowest walker was kept ahead, within sight.

## +30 · 王国卫剑

参考图：[王国卫剑](../../display/KnightSword/+30KnightSword.png)

### 武器描述

王国骑士用于巡行与驻防的银钢长剑。剑身中央镶有细金纹，护手两侧微微上扬，蓝色皮革握柄以金线交缠，护手和剑首的蓝石形成一套完整纹饰。修长双刃便于在骑行接敌时挥斩，也适合下马步战，是受封骑士身份与职责的标识。

### Weapon description

A silversteel longsword carried by the kingdom's knights on patrol and garrison duty. Fine gold inlay follows the blade's centre, the guard curves gently upward, and gold thread crosses a blue leather grip. Matching blue gems complete the guard and pommel. Its long edges serve in mounted combat and fighting on foot, marking a knight's station and charge.

### 故事片段

远征结束，奉命留守北境的渡口一带。巡行、护送粮车、搜寻失踪的商队，守到第三个冬天，随战报送来的还有一份举荐书。

洗净短甲上的泥，在城堡礼堂单膝跪下。领主的剑先后落在两肩，名册上的身份改作骑士。将王国的纹章扣在旧剑鞘上，再牵过拨来的战马，身后的士兵已经开始行礼。

次日照旧沿河巡行。经过村口，牵马的孩子仰着头，伸手碰了碰纹章。下马替他把松开的缰绳系好，才发现他的手背，也磨着练剑留下的水泡。

### Story fragment

The expedition ended with orders to hold the northern ferry crossings. Patrols, grain escorts, searches for missing caravans. By the third winter, a letter of recommendation arrived with the dispatches.

Mud washed from the mail coat; one knee on the floor of the castle hall. The lord's sword touched each shoulder. The muster roll was amended to read knight. The kingdom's crest fastened to the old scabbard, a newly assigned warhorse led forward. Behind, the soldiers had already begun to salute.

The next day brought the same patrol along the river. At a village entrance, a boy holding the horse craned upward and touched the crest. Down from the saddle to secure the loose reins. On the backs of his hands, the same blisters left by sword practice.

## +40 · 荣耀骑士剑

参考图：[荣耀骑士剑](../../display/KnightSword/+40KnightSword.png)

### 武器描述

久经战阵的统兵骑士佩剑，以精锻银钢成刃，细密金纹沿剑身中央延伸，护手如枝叶般托住深蓝宝石。双刃与剑脊均经反复淬炼，能够承受阵前挥斩与频繁格挡。金饰记录持剑者的战功，锋刃则用于冲阵、断后与守住退路。

### Weapon description

A battle-seasoned sword of commanding knights. Finely forged silversteel carries intricate gold inlay down the blade, while branching guardwork cradles a deep blue gem. Repeated tempering prepares the edges and ridge for hard cuts and frequent parries. Its goldwork records deeds of valour; its blade serves in charges, rearguard actions and the defence of retreating columns.

### 故事片段

余烬君王的军队越过北境，几座城堡接连失守。奉命在渡口上游的石桥断后，守到最后一队伤兵过河，带去的骑兵只回来十七人。桥上的赤火落进水里，沿着漂散的木板烧了一夜。

因守桥之功受封男爵，封地就在河口，几处村庄还没来得及收粮。敕书送到时，正与军吏清点伤亡。把阵亡者的姓名誊好，托返乡的人带给各家。

没能回封地受贺，便先下令开仓，安置过河的难民。家徽还未刻上剑鞘，新的征调令已经送进营里。

### Story fragment

The Sovereign of Ash's armies crossed the northern frontier. Castle after castle fell. Orders came to hold the stone bridge above the ferry. The last wounded crossed safely; only seventeen of the cavalry returned. Red fire fell from the bridge into the water and burned along the drifting timbers all night.

A barony for holding the bridge. The fief lay at the river mouth, its villages still waiting to harvest. The charter arrived during the casualty count. The names of the dead copied out and entrusted to travellers bound for their homes.

No return to the fief for celebrations. Orders went ahead to open the granaries and shelter refugees from across the river. Before the family crest could be carved into the scabbard, another summons reached the camp.

## +50 · 王庭圣剑

参考图：[王庭圣剑](../../display/KnightSword/+50KnightSword.png)

### 武器描述

汇集王庭锻造工艺的银钢长剑。刃根收成对称的棱角，金色星纹沿中央贯下，宽展护手与剑首均嵌有深蓝宝石。精选剑材经反复锻淬，保持锋利的同时承受重击，供王庭骑士在军阵前列作战。星纹是效忠王庭的印记，剑的威力仍出自锻工与持剑者的技艺。

### Weapon description

A silversteel longsword made with the royal court's finest craft. Angular shoulders frame the base of the blade; golden stars follow its centre, with deep blue gems in the broad guard and pommel. Carefully chosen metal, forged and tempered to hold an edge under heavy blows, equips court knights for the front rank. The stars mark allegiance. Its strength remains in the smith's craft and the wielder's skill.

### 故事片段

北境守军退过大河，奉召带着旧部赶到王都。各地骑士在宫前集结，编成讨伐余烬君王的军团。披上蓝披风，仍站在河口带回的十七人前面。

北上的路上，沿途驿站住满伤兵，每到一处便收拢还能行军的人。粮车越来越少，队伍却越来越长。回到旧渡口，岸边只剩烧黑的木桩。

统领每夜将佩剑横在膝上，照着旧册念历任将领的名字。临战前，第一次被叫到帐里，在地图上指出当年绕过河滩的那条小路。

### Story fragment

The northern garrisons fell back across the great river. A summons to the capital, the old company in tow. Knights from every province gathered before the palace, forming an army to march against the Sovereign of Ash. A blue cloak now, but still the same seventeen from the river mouth standing behind.

On the road north, every post station was full of wounded. At each stop, more who could still march joined the column. Fewer grain carts, more soldiers. Back at the old ferry, only charred piles remained along the bank.

Each night, the commander laid a sword across his knees and read the names of former commanders from an old roll. Before battle came the first summons into his tent. A finger traced the narrow path once taken around the riverbank on the map.

## +60 · 王庭誓约

参考图：[王庭誓约](../../display/KnightSword/+60KnightSword.png)

### 武器描述

承载历代统领英灵的银钢圣剑。层叠的金银翼饰围护蓝色圣石，誓词与金纹沿剑身相连，死去的持剑者将灵魂留在誓约之中。神力可随挥剑化作圣光，破开赤火，庇护跟随军旗而来的人。圣石每添一道金纹，便多了一位仍在履行誓言的守护者。

### Weapon description

A silversteel sacred blade bearing the spirits of generations of commanders. Layered wings of gold and silver shelter its blue sacred stone; oath inscriptions join the gold tracery along the blade. Fallen bearers leave their souls within the oath. Divine light follows its sweep, parting red fire and sheltering those who rally to the colours. Each new gold line in the stone marks another guardian still keeping a vow.

### 故事片段

沿旧路绕过渡口，统领率军突入敌营。帐后，暴君举起龙骸巨剑，炉心涌出的赤火转眼吞没前列。折返倒下的军旗下，从统领熔坏的手甲中接过王庭誓约。

剑上刻着旧册中那些名字，以及历代统领留下的誓词。每一任持剑者死后，英灵便随誓约留在剑中。蓝石深处，又一道金纹缓缓凝成。

握紧剑柄，圣光沿着刻纹漫出，赤火在剑前分开。散乱的士兵循光聚拢，将伤兵从沟里拖回。接过副官递来的残旗，直到最后一队人撤走，才转身离开。

### Story fragment

Around the ferry by the old path, the commander led the assault into the enemy camp. Behind the tents, the tyrant raised a dragonbone greatsword. Red fire poured from its furnace heart and swallowed the front rank. Back beneath the fallen banner; Royal Oathblade taken from the commander's melted gauntlet.

The names from the old roll were carved into the blade, beside the vows left by past commanders. Each bearer, upon death, left a spirit within the oath. Deep in the blue stone, another line of gold slowly formed.

The grip tightened. Sacred light spread through the inscriptions, and red fire parted before the blade. Scattered soldiers rallied to the light and dragged the wounded from the ditch. The adjutant handed over the torn banner. Only when the last company had withdrawn was it time to turn away.

## +70 · 黄金誓约

参考图：[黄金誓约](../../display/KnightSword/+70KnightSword.png)

### 武器描述

汇集诸军誓言的圣剑，延续王庭誓约中的英灵与圣石。银白剑刃中央铺开蓝底金纹，金翼环绕护手，细密誓纹连向剑首。亡者的誓约与生者的守土之誓共同催动神力，使圣光沿军阵展开，抵御暴君的赤火，为守军撑起接战与救援的屏障。

### Weapon description

A sacred blade gathering the vows of the armies, preserving the spirits and stone of Royal Oathblade. Gold tracery spreads over blue along the silver-white blade; golden wings surround the guard, fine oath inscriptions reaching toward the pommel. The pledges of the dead and the living's vow to defend their land stir its divine power. Sacred light spreads along the ranks, holding back the tyrant's fire while soldiers fight and recover their wounded.

### 故事片段

带着残军退回王都，各军将领共同推举，在殿前受任总帅。将残旗放在阶前，把当年河口的伤亡册连同统领的旧册，一起交给随军的书记官。

诸军在剑前立誓，旧纹间的金光一层层亮起。将残缺的军旗重新立起，各营收拢伤兵，在校场合编列阵。

余烬君王逼近时，持剑守在阵前。圣光挡住赤火，步兵趁隙接回伤员，再把缺口补齐。苦战三日，退到城下，点名的声音从白天持续到深夜。剑上已有裂痕，远处的炉心仍未熄灭。

### Story fragment

The remnants brought back to the capital. By the assembled commanders' nomination, an appointment as marshal before the throne. The torn banner laid at the steps; the casualty list from the river mouth and the commander's old roll handed together to the army scribe.

The armies swore before the blade. Gold light brightened between the old inscriptions. Torn standards raised again, the camps gathered their wounded and re-formed together on the parade ground.

As the Sovereign of Ash approached, the sword held the front. Sacred light checked the red fire. Infantry recovered the wounded in its shelter, then closed the gaps. Three bitter days before the retreat to the walls. Roll call lasted from daylight deep into the night. Cracks marked the sword. In the distance, the furnace heart still burned.

## +80～+90 · 最后的黎明

参考图：[最后的黎明](../../display/KnightSword/+80KnightSword.png)

### 武器描述

举国献上的银钢、王冠与骑士佩剑重铸而成的镇魔圣剑。原有圣石与历代誓词得以保留，完整的黄金圣环环抱深蓝宝石，银白剑身贯有日芒般的金纹。以众人的誓言凝聚封镇之力，为终结余烬君王的不灭魔焰而铸；封印闭合时，执剑者亦须留在其中。

### Weapon description

A sacred blade of binding, reforged from a nation's silversteel, its crown and its knights' swords. The original sacred stone and generations of vows endure. A complete golden halo embraces the deep blue gem, while sunray tracery runs through the silver-white blade. The gathered oaths give the seal its strength. Forged to end the Sovereign of Ash's undying flame; when the seal closes, the bearer must remain within it.

### 故事片段

各地送来的银钢与圣石堆满圣堂，王冠与骑士佩剑相继入炉。铁匠轮班掌锤，伤兵推着风箱，城头的守军苦撑了七夜。取回重铸的佩剑，那枚蓝石仍在，历代誓词之下，又添了各军献上的姓名。

在镇魔的誓约末尾签下姓名，将爵印与家徽留在祭坛，用布条把右手与剑柄缠紧。

迎着箭雨踏入焦土，折断扎进肩头的箭杆，继续向前。黑旗下，龙骸巨剑燃起赤火。举起最后的黎明，圣光映亮残破的甲胄，剑尖直指军阵中央的君王。

### Story fragment

Silversteel and sacred stones from every province filled the sanctuary. The crown and knights' swords went into the furnace together. Smiths took turns at the hammers, wounded soldiers worked the bellows, and the troops on the walls held for seven nights. The reforged sword returned with the same blue stone. Beneath the old vows, the names offered by the armies had been added.

A name signed at the foot of the binding oath. The seal of rank and family crest left on the altar. Cloth binding the right hand fast to the hilt.

Into the arrowstorm across the scorched earth. An arrow struck the shoulder; its shaft snapped short, and the advance continued. Beneath black banners, red fire kindled on the dragonbone greatsword. The Last Dawn rose, sacred light catching the ruined armour. Its point was levelled at the king in the heart of the ranks.
