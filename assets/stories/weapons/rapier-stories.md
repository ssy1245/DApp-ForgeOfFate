# 刺剑 · 武器描述与故事

状态：中英描述与故事讨论稿 v0.1 · 2026-10-26

按现有八个独立外观阶段组织 +5～+80 的中英描述与故事。沿用骑士剑的近身视角，省略主角的人称称谓；武器描述与经历分开，每段中文故事约一百至二百字，英文逐段对应。共用初始剑仍见骑士剑整合稿，本稿从精铁刺剑开始，不逐阶安排换剑或升级仪式。

故事从北境村庄的普通女孩出发，经过习武、入团、宫廷比剑与受封，转向绯翼骑士团护送平民、保存知识的使命。与骑士剑共享驿道、河口、十七名归来的骑兵、王都守城及余烬君王的赤火；少量相遇带出从小一同习武的关系，不替另一位持剑者写定最后的结局。

两条路线的强化节点不等同于同一日期：刺剑 +40 对应北境失守后的护送，+60 与骑士剑的王都守城阶段交会，+70～+80 延续撤离旅程。高阶圣石的庇护之力真实存在；羽翼由守护誓约显现，是否另有真正的天使仍留白。

## +5～+9 · 精铁刺剑

参考图：[精铁刺剑](../../display/Rapier/+5Rapier.png)

### 武器描述

以精炼铁料锻成的朴素细剑。窄直剑刃便于控制刺击方向，开放的弧形护手托住握剑的手，深棕皮柄以细绳缠紧，切面剑首用于平衡长刃。常用于练习步法、收势与格挡，也可随身护卫行旅，是初学者能够长久使用的兵器。

### Weapon description

A plain rapier forged from refined iron. The narrow, straight blade allows precise control of the point; an open, curved guard shelters the hand. Fine cord binds the dark brown leather grip, and a faceted pommel balances the long blade. Used for footwork, recovery and parrying drills, or protection on the road. A weapon an apprentice can grow accustomed to over many years.

### 故事片段

村子靠着北境驿道，母亲替过冬的驻军缝补衣裳。一把留下抵工钱的细剑，裹进旧布缝成的剑袋，剑首上还留着旧主人的刻痕。

送完衣裳，便去驻营的女骑士那里学剑。步子沿地上的炭线进退，剑尖对准廊下悬着的布结。铁匠家的孩子练完劈砍，也来比谁刺得准。

最初总把绳子碰得乱晃。又过一个冬天，布结终于能从正中穿过，炭线外也不再留下脚印。

### Story fragment

The village stood beside the northern post road. Mother mended clothes for the winter garrison. A rapier left in payment went into a bag sewn from old cloth, the former owner's marks still on its pommel.

After the clothes were delivered, lessons with a woman knight billeted in camp. Steps forward and back along charcoal lines, the point aimed at a cloth knot hanging beneath the eaves. After cutting practice, the smith's child came over too, to see whose thrust was truer.

At first, the cord swung wildly with every attempt. Another winter passed before the knot could be pierced through its centre, with no footprints left beyond the charcoal lines.

## +10 · 银锋刺剑

参考图：[银锋刺剑](../../display/Rapier/+10Rapier.png)

### 武器描述

银钢制成的修长刺剑，刃线收束至细尖，适合小幅拨挡后的迅速反击。素银护手向两侧弯展，中央饰有鸢尾盾徽，黑色皮柄与切面剑首保持简洁。骑士团见习成员常用此类佩剑修习护卫剑术，纹章标明随行的身份与纪律。

### Weapon description

A slender silversteel rapier, its edges tapering to a fine point for a swift answer after a small parry. Plain silver guardwork curves outward around an iris shield, with a simple black leather grip and faceted pommel. A pattern used by the order's apprentices to learn escort swordsmanship. Its crest identifies the company served and the discipline expected.

### 故事片段

驻军离村那天，教剑的女骑士留下了一封荐书。随补给队南下，进了绯翼骑士团的见习院。登记家世时，只报出村名和母亲开的缝衣铺，书记官核对后，在名册末尾添上一行。

院里有贵族的女儿，也有从各地军营挑来的学徒。白天练剑、骑马，夜里补学文字与礼仪，抄坏的纸留着记步法。

头一次随队穿过王都，队列从宫门排到街口。牵稳战马，走到换岗钟响，才轮到进院吃饭。

### Story fragment

When the garrison left the village, the knight who taught the lessons left a letter of recommendation. South with the supply train, then into the apprentice quarters of the Order of the Crimson Wing. For family particulars, only a village and Mother's sewing shop. The clerk checked the details and added a line at the foot of the roll.

The quarters housed nobles' daughters and apprentices chosen from distant garrisons. Sword and riding lessons by day, letters and etiquette at night. Spoiled copy sheets kept for notes on footwork.

The first procession through the capital stretched from the palace gates to the street corner. Horses held steady, the walk continued until the changing bell. Only then, a turn to enter the courtyard and eat.

## +20 · 宫廷细剑

参考图：[宫廷细剑](../../display/Rapier/+20Rapier.png)

### 武器描述

供宫廷比剑与随行护卫使用的银钢细剑。月桂沿双弧护手展开，少量暖金嵌进剑根与鸢尾盾框，修长的刃身兼顾触感与韧性。握柄便于调整剑尖角度，在狭窄距离内拨开来刃、寻隙刺入，也能在正式仪典中保持收敛的姿态。

### Weapon description

A silversteel rapier for court bouts and close escort duty. Laurel leaves follow the twin curves of the guard, with touches of warm gold at the blade's base and around the iris shield. The long blade balances responsiveness with resilience. Its grip allows fine changes of angle to deflect a blade and find an opening at close quarters, while retaining the restraint expected at ceremony.

### 故事片段

春季的宫廷比剑在花园举行，长廊挂满各家的纹章。骑士团荐送见习者参试，借来的礼服绷着肩，剑带上还留着雨水浸过的旧印。

连过三场，最后一场的对手总从右侧抢攻。退半步，让开剑锋，再将细尖送进胸前的布结。裁判的铃响后，先收剑，才把蹭开的袖口拉好。

赛后调入宫廷护卫，白日随侍出行，夜里熟记宫道与换防。此前抄了许多遍的名字，终于能在亲自递来的通行帖上认全。

### Story fragment

The spring court bouts were held in the gardens, the galleries hung with family crests. The order nominated apprentices to compete. Borrowed formal clothes pulled tight at the shoulders; old rain stains still marked the sword belt.

Three bouts won. The final opponent always pressed from the right. Half a step back, clear of the blade, then the fine point through the cloth knot at the chest. At the judge's bell, the sword lowered before the rumpled cuff was straightened.

A posting to the palace escort followed. Attendance by day, palace routes and watch rotations learnt by night. Names copied so many times could at last be read in full on the passes handed over in person.

## +30 · 鸢尾刺剑

参考图：[鸢尾刺剑](../../display/Rapier/+30Rapier.png)

### 武器描述

绯翼骑士团正式骑士的银钢佩剑。鸢尾盾徽被低冠与月桂环护，浅金镶线沿银白剑身向上延伸，黑色皮柄保留单手持握的修长比例。用于护送使团、巡察驿路与贴身守卫，锋利剑尖便于制住近身威胁，盾徽则是受托护卫的凭证。

### Weapon description

A silversteel rapier of the Order of the Crimson Wing's sworn knights. A low crown and laurel frame the iris shield; pale gold inlay rises along the silver-white blade, above a slender black leather grip. Carried on diplomatic escorts, post-road patrols and close protection duties. The keen point checks threats at close range; the shield signifies an entrusted charge.

### 故事片段

受封仪式在王室礼堂举行，依次报出姓名、籍贯与服役年限。单膝跪下，念完誓词，将鸢尾徽记缀在披风上，随后领下独立带队的差遣。

第一次护送官文北上，驻地就在河口。营门前，铁匠家的少年已披上骑士的斗篷，旧剑鞘上扣着王国的纹章。卸下行囊，将家乡捎来的干饼放在桌边。

入夜才有空并排坐下。炉房已经修好，村道也铺了新石。话说到一半，巡行的号声响起，两把佩剑又各自挂回腰间。

### Story fragment

The investiture took place in the royal hall. Names, birthplaces and years of service read in turn. One knee lowered, the oath spoken, the iris badge sewn to the cloak. Then an independent escort assignment.

The first dispatch convoy north was bound for the river mouth. At the camp gate, the smith's boy now wore a knight's cloak, the kingdom's crest fastened to the old scabbard. Pack set down, hard bread brought from home placed at the table's edge.

Only after nightfall, time to sit side by side. The forge had been repaired; new stone laid on the village road. Halfway through the news, the patrol horn sounded. Two swords went back to their separate belts.

## +40 · 王室誓剑

参考图：[王室誓剑](../../display/Rapier/+40Rapier.png)

### 武器描述

承载王室护卫誓言的银钢刺剑。完整冠冕立在鸢尾盾徽之上，金色月桂与细长镶纹连向剑身，弧形护手保留迅速转腕的余地。细尖擅于抢占窄处、守住车旁与门前的空隙，常随奉王命护送平民的骑士出行，冠饰使沿途驻军识别其使命。

### Weapon description

A silversteel rapier bearing the royal oath of protection. A full crown stands above the iris shield, joined to the blade by golden laurel and slender inlay. Curved guardwork leaves room for quick turns of the wrist. The fine point commands narrow approaches beside wagons and doorways. Carried by knights escorting civilians under royal orders, its crown identifies their charge to the garrisons along the road.

### 故事片段

北境失守的战报送进王都，石桥上的骑兵只回来十七人。庭中的庆典撤下，地图换成渡口与粮道。王室将护送平民的命令交给绯翼骑士团，玺印压在名册与通行文书上。

带队赶到河岸，桥面已断，岸边挤满等船的人。先让伤病者过河，再卸下马鞍，把驮具改绑在粮车上，能多腾出几个位置。

最后一艘船靠岸时，天色已经发白。将名字补进同行的名册，洗去通行印上的灰，继续往下一个村庄赶。

### Story fragment

Dispatches of the northern collapse reached the capital. Only seventeen riders had returned from the stone bridge. Celebrations were struck down, maps replaced with ferry crossings and grain roads. The royal charge to escort civilians passed to the Order of the Crimson Wing, the seal pressed onto rolls and travel warrants.

At the river with the escort, the bridge was broken and the bank crowded with people waiting for boats. Sick and wounded across first. Saddles removed, packs lashed to the grain wagons to make a few more places.

The last boat reached the bank as the sky paled. New names added to the company roll, ash washed from the seal. On to the next village.

## +50 · 绯冠刺剑

参考图：[绯冠刺剑](../../display/Rapier/+50Rapier.png)

### 武器描述

饰有王冠红宝石的护卫刺剑。鎏金冠笼包住剑首的大颗红石，银色鸢尾盾徽与金色月桂沿护手相接，银钢长刃仍以准确刺击为用。红石是绯翼骑士团所守誓约的见证，冠饰标明护送者的通行权，使这柄佩剑既能应战，也能在封闭的城门前表明来意。

### Weapon description

An escort rapier bearing a ruby of the crown. A gilded cage encloses the large red stone at the pommel; the silver iris shield joins golden laurel along the guard. The silversteel blade remains a weapon of precise thrusts. Its ruby witnesses the order's pledge, its crown denotes the escort's right of passage. A blade for combat, and a sign of authority before a closed city gate.

### 故事片段

带回王都的难民住满街巷，骑士团奉命筹备继续向内陆撤离。接管车队时，贵族行箱已经堆满半院，城门外还有匠人与孩子等着登记。

按随行人数重排车辆，拆下多余的座椅，给病人铺上草垫。王室书库也打开了，医书、农书与工匠图册逐箱装车，最重的几卷留给空着手的骑士背负。

从前练字用过的识字课本放在最上面。出发前，让认得字的孩子围过来，照着名单一个个点人，再将读完的书包好，交给下一辆车。

### Story fragment

Refugees brought to the capital filled its streets. The order was charged with preparing a further withdrawal inland. On taking over the wagons, half the courtyard was already stacked with nobles' travelling chests. Artisans and children still waited outside the gate to be registered.

Vehicles reassigned by the number travelling, spare seats removed, straw laid down for the sick. The royal library opened too. Medical books, farming manuals and craftsmen's drawings packed by the chest; the heaviest volumes strapped to knights with free hands.

The primer once used for writing lessons placed on top. Before departure, children who could read gathered to call the names, one by one. The book wrapped again and passed to the next wagon.

## +60 · 赤蔷誓约

参考图：[赤蔷誓约](../../display/Rapier/+60Rapier.png)

### 武器描述

因守护誓约而显现神力的银钢细剑。赤红圣石嵌在护手中央，星形红纹沿刃根与握柄呼应，黄金枝饰向两侧舒展，剑首仍保留冠笼中的红宝石。誓词催动时，光沿镶线连成庇护的薄障，隔开飞来的邪焰，为身后的伤者与车队争取通过险地的时间。

### Weapon description

A silversteel rapier awakened by its oath of protection. A red sacred stone centres the guard; red stars answer one another along the blade's base and grip. Golden branches spread outward, the crowned ruby remaining at the pommel. With the oath, light joins along the inlay to form a thin ward, turning aside hostile flame and buying passage for the wounded and wagons behind.

### 故事片段

城中守军重新列阵时，收拢最后一批孩子与匠人。几辆车撤去王室装饰，车厢铺满书箱，病人坐在上面，识字的孩子照着名册应答。

临行前在军帐见到总帅。将撤离路线划向山外，他把残旗压在王都。把一卷家乡带来的裹伤布留在案上，接过通行印信，便各自去清点剩下的人。

出城后，赤火从追兵的箭上落下。举剑挡在车前，红石亮起，光沿誓纹连成薄障。最后一辆车通过，才翻身追上队伍。

### Story fragment

While the city's defenders re-formed, the last children and artisans were gathered. Royal ornaments removed from several wagons, book chests covering the beds. The sick seated on top, children who could read answering from the roll.

Before departure, a meeting with the marshal in the command tent. The evacuation route traced beyond the mountains; his torn flag laid over the capital. A roll of bandage cloth from home left on the table, the travel seal taken up. Separate counts now of those who remained.

Outside the city, red fire fell from the pursuers' arrows. Sword raised before the wagons, the red stone brightened, light joining along the oath inscriptions into a thin ward. Only when the last wagon passed, back into the saddle to catch the column.

## +70～+80 · 炽翼天使

参考图：[炽翼天使](../../display/Rapier/+70Rapier.png)

### 武器描述

绯翼骑士团守护誓约的最终圣器。银白羽翼沿护手与剑根展开，黄金纹饰连贯至长刃，赤红圣石与冠笼中的宝石共同维系神力。挥剑时，翼形圣光可覆住近旁的同行者，抵御箭雨与魔焰；细刃仍能迎击逼近的追兵。人们以天使称呼这份庇护，将它留给仍需走下去的人。

### Weapon description

The final sacred form of the Order of the Crimson Wing's protective oath. Silver-white wings unfold along the guard and blade's base, golden tracery rising along the long blade. Red sacred stones and the crowned gem sustain its divine power. Wing-shaped light shelters nearby companions from arrows and sorcerous fire, while the fine blade meets pursuers at close range. Those beneath its shelter call it an angel, a protection carried onward by the living.

### 故事片段

翻过山口的第二夜，追兵截住谷道。车轮卡在碎石里，前队还在推车，后队的骑士已经拔剑。将惊马牵到岩壁边，再把落队的孩子送进车阵。

最后一盏灯被箭射灭，红石却愈发明亮。圣光沿剑锋展开，化作覆住车队的白翼。箭矢折在光外，火星落进碎石。踏进追兵与车轮之间，守到道路终于让开。

天亮后，在废弃的驿站摊开识字课本。孩子们围着书箱读第一页，轮值的骑士守在门外。通往王都的驿路上，迟迟没有归来的马蹄声。

### Story fragment

On the second night beyond the pass, pursuers blocked the ravine. Wheels caught in scree, the front escort still pushing while the rearguard drew swords. The frightened horses led against the rock face; straggling children brought inside the circle of wagons.

An arrow struck out the last lamp. The red stone only brightened. Sacred light spread along the blade, becoming white wings over the wagons. Arrows broke against the light; sparks fell among the stones. Into the space between pursuers and wheels, holding until the road was clear.

At dawn, the primer opened in an abandoned post station. Children gathered around the book chest to read the first page, knights taking watch outside the door. Along the post road from the capital, still no returning hoofbeats.
